H1 · The Judge FILED — HYPOTHESIS REJECTED

Is Foul Play’s evaluator better than ours?

No — it’s much worse, and that’s the finding. FP’s judge is a ~16-term hand-written heuristic (flat +10 for “holding any item”, no move knowledge, no speed awareness) scoring AUC 0.54 — near coin-flip — vs our 0.75. FP structurally can’t swap it (no seam in the published engine). We already run the strictly better judge and lose anyway. An old isolated experiment (same searcher, judge swapped, 600 paired games each) produced bit-identical win counts — the eval axis is closed as a primary lane.

⚠ The lead it produced instead: FP’s source shows it spawns 2–4 parallel search processes — each with the FULL 100ms — on high-uncertainty turns (team preview, few revealed moves). On the turns that decide games, “equal budget” may quietly be 2–4× in FP’s favor. Directly testable in one run. Also: our 214-feature judge reads zero item/ability information — even FP’s dumb heuristic reads Boots and Magic Guard.

Recommends: (1) the never-run decomposition of the +8pp stack; (2) measure/equalize FP’s per-phase world count; (3) stop investing in judge tuning until items/abilities reach the features.

H2 · The Belief Layer FILED — SMOKING GUN CANDIDATE

Is our opponent-inference feeding the search fantasy worlds?

Yes — measurably, badly. At matched one-world search (FP’s multi-world mode is off in our benchmarks — same engine, same budget, same K), the difference is what the one world is made of. FP samples one complete, real, internally-consistent opponent set from usage data. We assemble ours from three independent guesses (ability, item, moves picked separately) — which builds rules-violating opponents (Assault Vest Primarina running Calm Mind, found repeatedly in tonight’s corpus) and, decisively: the moveset we searched was missing a move the opponent later actually used in ~41% of contested decisions. Two of five hard decisions, we deep-searched a fantasy.

And “belief is our advantage” has never been tested — every mention in the docs is motivation for a sweep that never ran.

Already building tonight: the coherent-world fix (stand the searched world on a real complete set + a legality filter that rejects impossible combos) — the candidate arm for tomorrow’s needle-mover sim. Plus the never-run no-belief counterfactual goes on the sim queue.
